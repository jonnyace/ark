Water cycle

The water cycle is the continuous movement of water between the oceans, the air and the land.

Stages

Water evaporates from oceans, lakes and plants, condenses into clouds, and falls as rain or snow. On land it runs off into rivers, soaks into the ground as groundwater, or is taken up by plants, and eventually returns to the sea.

Groundwater

Groundwater fills the spaces in soil and rock. Wells draw on it. Shallow wells can be contaminated by floodwater or nearby septic systems, so well water may need testing after a flood.
