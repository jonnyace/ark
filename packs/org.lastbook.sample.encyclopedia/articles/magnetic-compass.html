Magnetic compass

A magnetic compass has a magnetised needle that turns to line up with the Earth's magnetic field, so one end points towards magnetic north.

Declination

Magnetic north is not the same as true (geographic) north. The angle between them is called declination and changes from place to place and slowly over time. Paper maps usually print the local declination in the margin.

Using a compass with a map

To follow a bearing, turn the map so its north matches the compass after correcting for declination, then pick a landmark in the direction of travel and walk to it.
